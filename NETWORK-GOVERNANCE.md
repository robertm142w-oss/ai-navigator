# Founder Control and Network Governance
Status: proposed requirements; implementation and verification pending.

Use server-enforced authentication and role-based authorization for connected services. Establish a verified owner role with MFA, secure recovery and least-privilege delegated administrators. Support account suspension/revocation, device credential revocation, scoped permissions, session invalidation, rate limits and emergency suspension of network services. Use short-lived tokens and server-side revocation checks; document offline limitations and distinguish local app use from network access. Never remotely erase personal data or disable unrelated device functions. Disclose access policies to users, minimize data collection, provide proportionate abuse review and appeal/reinstatement, and maintain protected audit logs. Do not rely on secret client-side passwords or hidden backdoors. Protect governance, authentication, signing and approval boundaries from AI-generated changes; require independent review, tests and explicit authorized approval. Keep owner recovery independent of AI. Test unauthorized role escalation, revoked sessions/devices, offline behavior, recovery, audit integrity and emergency restoration. Prioritize existing AI core verification before network rollout. No paid commitments without approval.
